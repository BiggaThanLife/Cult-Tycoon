## Required changes

Text replacements are ASCII and no longer than the original.

### 1. `room.bunk_room.desc` (text, owner: narrative-writer)
"Entirely optional." in this deadpan voice reads as an ironic denial, i.e. that living in the Bunk Room is *not*
optional. That implies compulsory residence, which brief point 1 and the TONE "Don't" (followers locked in) rule out.
Move the joke onto the leader's hypocrisy instead.
- Now (46): `Cozy beds. Entirely optional. Quilts included.`
- New (45): `Cozy bunks. The Prophet's mattress is bigger.`

### 2. `room_kitchen` sprite: soup colour (art, owner: pixel-artist)
The stockpot is a round, dark pot (`ring(16, 13, 16, PAL.ink2)`) filled with `PAL.acid` (#b6ff7a, bright lime) with
white bubbles and steam. Lime liquid bubbling in a round dark pot is the stock "toxic brew / cauldron" image, and this
pot feeds the whole flock and changes a mental stat. That sits too close to the communal-drink and covert-dosing hard
limits (CLAUDE.md, D-110). It is soup; it should look like soup.
- In `p.r(17, 15, 14, 2, PAL.acid)` change `PAL.acid` to `PAL.wood` (warm broth).
- Change the two bubble pixels `px(20, 15, PAL.white)` and `px(26, 16, PAL.white)` to `PAL.grass` (basil flecks).
- Keep the steam pixels (white/bone). No skulls, no labels on the jars.
- Recommended, not required: a straight-sided stockpot with two side handles, or a ladle handle standing in the pot
  (e.g. `r(29, 10, 1, 5, PAL.ink2)`), to move the silhouette further from a cauldron.

### 3. `room_reading_room` sprite: the eyes in the shelf (art, owner: pixel-artist)
Drawn as `r(13, 15, 3, 6, PAL.ink).px(13, 17, PAL.acid).px(15, 17, PAL.acid)`, commented "a gap, something is looking
out". In the room followers are sent to lower their Doubt, an unidentified watcher in the dark reads as surveillance of
the readers and leans creepy. The eyes are already the compound cat's eye colour (`cat` uses `PAL.acid` for `g`), so make
it unmistakably the cat, which is the game's established comic prophet and cozy, not menacing:
- add a pink nose: `px(14, 18, PAL.pink)`;
- add a tail hanging out of the gap over the shelf below: `r(16, 21, 1, 3, PAL.ink).px(17, 24, PAL.ink)`;
- change the comment to "the cat, asleep on the pamphlets".
Acceptable alternative: remove the two eye pixels and fill the gap with one leaning pamphlet (`PAL.robeL`).

### 4. Reading Room keeps working while sealed (mechanic, owner: systems-engineer)
`tickSanctum` `case 'reading_room'` lowers readers' Doubt without checking `groupSealed`. Every other room stops while
sealed (`groupMult` returns 0), and `settleInspection` can seal a Reading Room (only Bunk Rooms are excluded). Result:
followers keep "reading" behind PENDING REVIEW tape. That is the closest this batch comes to people shut inside a
sealed room (brief point 1; guardrails section 1 rules 10-11). Fix: skip the Reading Room branch while
`groupSealed(run, grp)` is true (readers are "on paid leave" like every other sealed room's staff, per the spec), and
add a unit test for it.

## The seven brief points

1. **Bunk Rooms and the Reading Room: APPROVED after changes 1, 3 and 4.** Doubt falling in the Reading Room is
   acceptable as leader-vanity satire: CLAUDE.md and the GDD already list the Pamphlet-Reading Room as the soft
   alternative to harm, and the mechanic is persuasion by a bad pamphlet (a tactic the game satirises), not a drink
   (D-110 bans drinks erasing doubt, not reading). Framing rules: the joke is always the pamphlet and the Prophet
   (one pamphlet, his face on the cover), never the reader; the room is drawn as a cozy reading nook (chairs, lamp, no
   door, bars or locks); assignment is a normal job the player can undo at any time with no penalty; it never stops a
   follower from defecting; it never happens behind seal tape (change 4); a follower who helped an inquiry is never
   sent there (spec acceptance test 6). The visible cost (readers produce nothing) is the satirical edge TONE asks for.
   Recommended (designer/balancer call, not required): a Doubt floor of about 10 in the Reading Room, so the pamphlet
   never fully convinces anyone. Keep the player-facing name "Reading Room".
2. **Kitchen: APPROVED (text, data, mechanic); art CHANGES REQUIRED (change 2).** It is soup, made from a garden herb,
   served to everyone openly, and it raises Sanity, which is a comedy stat here. Condition: food never changes Doubt,
   Devotion or memory.
3. **Records Office / Filed Forms: APPROVED.** Completed paperwork softening a civil inspection is exactly the D-149
   compliance-theatre joke. Forms are produced by the office, not handed over as gifts. Note B.
4. **Wellness Retreat: APPROVED.** Grift satire with a built-in cost (Suspicion), the joke lands on the invoice. Note D.
5. **Ritual Chamber art: APPROVED.** Two candles on stands with 3-pixel flames, teal glow, a floor chalk ellipse, an
   eye-diamond glyph and invented wall marks. The marks do not match real runic letters or hate symbols; there is no
   pentagram, inverted cross or other real religious sign, and no fire beyond the candle tips.
6. **Storeys upward: APPROVED.** Growth is above ground with a tiled roof, sky, scaffolding and exposed brick; zoning
   jokes target the leader; height costs Suspicion ("visible from the road"). Nothing is underground, fortified,
   hidden or called a bunker or shelter.
7. **Reading-room eyes: CHANGES REQUIRED (change 3).** As an unidentified "something looking out" over the readers it
   reads as surveillance and is too creepy for the followers' space; as the compound cat (nose and tail added) it is
   cute-but-wrong and fine.

## Notes and recommendations (not required)

- **A. `sanctum.unassign` "Remove".** Fine in a job-slot UI. If a writer wants to avoid "remove" next to a person
  (guardrails: "nobody is removed"), `Excuse` (6) fits the length limit.
- **B. Filed Forms.** Keep them *completed, honest* paperwork: never forged, backdated, shredded or given to officials
  as a gift ("Thank you, no. It's policy."). Forms also shorten a volunteer helper's time away (`awaySec`); that is fine
  as "less to go through", but no text may suggest the leader used forms to stop a follower helping the inquiry.
- **C. Kitchen.** Never route potions or Alchemy output through the Kitchen; follower potion use needs its own spec
  (D-144).
- **D. Wellness Retreat art.** The lotus-over-water sign is the generic commercial spa pictogram. The lotus is sacred
  in Hinduism and Buddhism (Britannica), but it is an open, widely used symbol, not a closed rite. Keep it a plain
  flower sign: no seated meditating figure, Buddha statue, Om or other script, chakra charts, mudras or bindi. The
  guests are paying visitors; no potions are offered there without the D-144 spec.
- **E. Eye emblem** (Main Hall banner, roof cap). Keep it out of a triangle with rays: that is the Eye of Providence,
  used in Christian art, on the US Great Seal and by Freemasonry (a real, living group).
- **F. Reading Room walls.** After change 3 the dark navy room with a pendant lamp reads as a cozy library. If QA's
  screenshots still read it as gloomy beside the bright Bunk Room, warm the wall (e.g. `PAL.robeD` / `PAL.robe` stripes)
  rather than adding props.
